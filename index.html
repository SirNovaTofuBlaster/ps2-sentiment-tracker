                  PS2 Price Index

                  Like a stock market index for PS2 games: it started at 100, and each day it moves by the typical change in each game's median asking price since the last day that game was priced (up to three days before). A game needs at least three copies listed; a median that more than doubled or halved is left out, as that is nearly always a change in what the search finds, though a smaller change of search words can still nudge it. Games joining or leaving never move it, and a cheap game counts as much as a dear one. US and UK are separate, consoles are not in it, and a day counts once it is over (UTC).
